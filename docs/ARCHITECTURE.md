# Architecture
- presentation: Compose UI, navigation, accessibility and localization.
- domain: pure Kotlin models and calculation contracts.
- data: Room persistence, ephemeris adapters, research source catalogue.
- panchang: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise/sunset and muhurta.
- vedic: chart, divisional charts, Vimshottari dasha, transit and yoga.
- numerology: versioned Indian/Vedic, Chaldean and Pythagorean methods.
- palmistry: image quality, visible line annotation and traditional reference text.
- tarot: unbiased random draw, spreads and documented card meanings.
- samudrika: face landmark visualization only; no identity/sensitive inference.
- remedies: source-linked puja, mantra, daan and gemstone tradition entries.
- verification: timestamped forecast/outcome ledger and calibration.

Every astronomical result must retain coordinates, timezone, UTC timestamp, ayanamsha, ephemeris/provider version, panchang tradition/source edition and rounding conventions. Astronomical calculation must remain separate from traditional interpretation.
