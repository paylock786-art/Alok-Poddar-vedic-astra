# Product Scope
1. Vedic astrology: birth chart, bhava, navamsha/divisional charts, graha, yoga, shadbala, Vimshottari dasha, transit and prashna.
2. Mithila Panchang first, with explicit comparison to other almanac traditions and source edition metadata.
3. Numerology: Indian/Vedic ank-jyotish, Chaldean and Pythagorean systems separately labelled; birth number, destiny number, name number, compound numbers and number grids.
4. Palmistry AI: consent-based palm photo quality checks and visible-line annotation.
5. Tarot: full 78-card deck, upright/reversed meanings and spreads.
6. Samudrika/face reading: visual landmark annotation only; no identity or sensitive-trait inference.
7. Remedies: puja, mantra, daan, muhurta and gemstone traditions with sources and non-coercive presentation.
8. Time windows: next 1/3/6/12/24 hours plus daily, weekly, monthly and annual views.
9. Combined report: preserve each system's separate findings and disclose disagreements.
10. Prediction verification: timestamped forecast and outcome records, sample size and calibration.
11. Hindi, Maithili and English localization.

## Guardrails
These are traditional belief systems, not scientifically established future prediction. Never guarantee outcomes or substitute for medical, legal, financial or safety advice. Gemstone and ritual information must not use fear-based sales tactics.
