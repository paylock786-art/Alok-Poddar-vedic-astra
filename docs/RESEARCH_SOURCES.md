# Research Source Policy
Candidate texts: Vedanga Jyotisha, Surya Siddhanta, Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Brihat Jataka and Phaladeepika. These are research candidates, not verified in-app citations yet.

Each source record must include exact title, author/tradition, editor/translator, publisher, edition/year, page/chapter/verse, language, licence/copyright status and the claim it supports.

Mithila Panchang rules must be checked against actual published editions. Never invent verse numbers, quotations, page references or institutional endorsements.
